# Sanctuary OS — Daily Dev Build v0.6.0-Dev.1
### Liquid Glass Surface Unification, Smarter Malware Scanning, Studio Guides & Mac Downloads
**Release Date:** October 28, 2026 | **Version:** 0.6.0-Dev.1

Welcome to **Sanctuary OS v0.6.0-Dev.1**! This Daily Dev Build focuses on making every card easier to read, scanning downloaded mods even more thoroughly, adding plain-English guides and visual icon pickers to our creator studios, and letting guides and legal docs update automatically from the cloud without needing an app update.

---

## ⚡ TL;DR — The 60-Second Summary

* **Full-Width Mod Titles & Cleaner Badges:** We moved card action buttons to the bottom-right corner across all queues and alert panels so mod titles never get cut off, and gave every status badge a calm, uniform glass look.
* **Floating Mobile Dock & Swipeable Tabs:** On phones and mobile web apps, the bottom navigation bar now floats like a rounded glass dock with smooth swipeable top tabs and no white bar at the bottom of Android screens.
* **Built-In Studio Guides & Visual Icon Picker:** The Game Schema and Language Pack studios now include an interactive field-by-field help guide and a searchable **Icon Picker** so you never have to guess icon names.
* **Deeper Archive & Package Scanning:** Our built-in malware scanner now looks inside compressed `.package` streams and nested `.zip` archives before importing, and tracks when a blocked file was moved into quarantine.
* **Mac Downloads, Web Donations & Beta Switcher:** Added native **Mac (.dmg)** Stable and Dev Build downloads, website donation verification (**$5 = +30 days** of Dev Builds), and a 1-click **Public vs. Beta** switcher for Web and Mobile.
* **Guided Feedback, Human Contact & Live Guides:** Added step-by-step Feedback, Bug Report (with automatic log attachment), Direct Human Contact, and Partner Community Application drawers—plus live cloud-synced guides and roadmaps.

---

## 🎮 For Players: Easier-to-Read Cards, Mobile Polish & Mac Support

* **Room for Long Mod Names:** Action buttons like *Update*, *Modules*, *Ignore*, *Delete*, and *Add* now sit in the bottom-right footer of every card across your Signature Queue, Update alerts, and Conflict panels—giving mod titles the full width of the card.
* **Clearer Multi-Issue Tooltips & Module Counts:** When a mod has multiple warnings, hovering its badge now lists each issue cleanly on its own line. Sub-module counts now appear right in the subtitle with a compact tree button in the corner.
* **Floating Glass Mobile Dock:** Mobile players now get a rounded, floating 3D glass bottom dock and a 2-at-a-time horizontal tab carousel across every screen, with full dark/theme color support on Android gesture bars.
* **Faster Nexus & Mod Library Loading:** Browsing **The Nexus** and opening large mod catalogs now loads multiple pages in parallel with smart caching, so mod lists appear almost instantly.
* **Native Mac Downloads & Smart Auto-Updater:** You can now download both the **Stable** and **Supporter Dev Build** for **Mac (.dmg)** right from the home page, and clicking **Install & Restart** automatically installs the right update for Windows or Mac.
* **Web Donation Verification & Beta Switcher:** Donating on the website now includes a 1-click **Verify Completed Donation** drawer to immediately credit your **+30 days per $5**, plus a toggle to switch the Website and Mobile app between **Public** and **Beta / Dev** streams.

---

## 🛡️ Security & Safety: Deeper Mod Scanning & Clear Quarantine Tracking

* **Scans Inside Packages & Nested Zips:** Before a downloaded mod is added to your library, Sanctuary OS now inspects inside compressed `.package` files, script archives, and zips-within-zips to catch hidden scripts or disguised malware.
* **Accurate Move-to-Quarantine Status:** When you import mods using **Move** mode and a threat is intercepted, Sanctuary OS moves the file out of your Downloads folder into quarantine and clearly labels it **Moved to Quarantine** instead of *Exposed on Disk*.
* **Detailed, Privacy-Safe Threat Reports:** Quarantine reports now show helpful safety details—like which inner file triggered the alert, the file type, and file size—without ever collecting or sharing any personal computer information.
* **Safer Mod Unlinking & Faster Backup Sizing:** Improved how the app cleans up mod links when switching folders, and sped up how Time Capsule calculates backup folder sizes in the background.

---

## 🛠️ For Creators & Community Leaders: Studio Guides, Icon Picker & Partner Tools

* **Interactive Guide for Schemas & Language Packs:** Not sure what a setting does? Click the new **Guide** button inside the Game Schema Studio or Language Pack Studio to browse plain-English explanations for every single field, plus a 21-term Glossary Cheat Sheet.
* **Searchable Visual Icon Override Builder:** Want to swap out any icon in the app for your language or theme pack? Pick the icon you want to change from a searchable dropdown and choose its replacement visually—no more guessing icon codes.
* **Custom Mod Categories per Game:** Mod categories and subcategories (like *Build/Buy* and *CAS* for Sims, or *Vehicles* and *Cyberware* for other games) are now customizable right inside each game's Schema settings, and creator dropdowns always include a search bar.
* **Cleaner Spoke Hub & Language Previews:** Fixed duplicate Schema and Language cards in Spoke Control, updated cards to show your community's name instead of *Sanctuary Foundry*, and hid noisy system audit logs from the Recent overview.
* **Auto-Filled Bug Reports & Direct Human Contact:** Reporting a platform bug now automatically fills in your app version, OS, and privacy-scrubbed logs (with an *Edit* button if you want to change anything). Need help? Reach a real human on our team—never an AI bot.
* **Partner Community Agreements & Live Cloud Docs:** Communities applying to host a Game Spoke can now read and accept the **Partners Agreement** and **Compliance Protocol** right inside the application flow. Plus, all guides, legal docs, and our **Phase 14 Roadmap** now sync live from the cloud without waiting for an app update.
