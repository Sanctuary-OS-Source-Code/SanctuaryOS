### Cheat-Proof Homestead Lab, Immortal Save Airgap & Native Fullscreen Chimes

- **Save-Verify-and-Rewind Lab Airgap (Zero Tainted Saves):** All Homestead airgap storage now lives completely outside your game's user-data folder inside an immortal Vault backup mirror. Your dedicated **Lab Test Save** is sealed read-only as a **Golden Baseline**: each test mounts a fresh disposable working copy so the game can perform a real save write during Stage 4 (checking ZIP, GZIP, Zstd, SQLite, JSON, and XML headers for save corruption), and then **discards the working copy on exit** so your test save never accumulates mod leftovers or Save-As clutter.
- **100% Anti-Cheat Safe, Flicker-Free & Pause-Aware Timer:** Removed transparent HUD overlays, global keyboard hooks, and process-memory scraping so Homestead Lab is completely invisible to DRM and kernel anti-cheats across Windows, macOS, and Linux/SteamOS—while eliminating DirectX 11 fullscreen flicker. The Stage 3 timer now waits until multi-stage world/lot loading screens settle completely, suspends automatically during secondary zone loads or `ESC` pause menus, and never false-pauses while you watch live gameplay animations.
- **Native Fullscreen Audio Chimes & One-Click Sandbox Teardown:** Playing fullscreen? Sanctuary synthesizes native OS audio chimes when your Stage 3 test timer begins, when it finishes (`Save Your Game Now to Finish`), and when Stage 4 confirms a clean save. Clicking **End Test** or **Abort** runs an atomic 1-click teardown that closes the game cleanly, restores your live saves, and re-projects your active Play Set—staging only the single mod and its required twin without pulling unrelated collection siblings.
- **2-Column Environment Builder, Pinned Known Errors & Newest-First Reports:** Redesigned **Environment Builder** with a compact searchable Vault grid on the left and a sticky **Active Test Bench** (`Primary`, `Dependencies`, `Conflict Check`) on the right. Overhauled **Known Errors** into 3 pinned categories (**Corrupted**, **Unstable**, **Unrelated**) with live crash-log paste diagnosis, ordered **Lab Reports** strictly **Most Recent to Oldest**, and gated **Submit to Architects** strictly to verified Tier 0 catalog hashes.
