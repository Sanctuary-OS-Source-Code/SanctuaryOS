### 9-Second 78 GB Time Capsules, 1-Click Symlink Unlock & Instant Collection Equipping

- **Parallel Copy-on-Write Time Capsules (78.05 GB Sealed in ~9s / Restored in ~11s):** Time Capsule snapshots are 100% file-format agnostic—classifying, hardlinking, and streaming any game's multi-gigabyte engine archives and world files (`>= 4 MB`—whether `.pak`, `.ba2`, `.bsa`, `.archive`, `.bundle`, `.vpk`, `.xnb`, `.package`, or binaries) in parallel across all CPU cores with real-time byte-weighted progress. Sealing a **78.05 GB Engine Core (`5,120` files: `35.96 GB` new delta + `42.09 GB` / `54%` zero-byte CoW hardlinks)** finishes in **~9 seconds**, and restoring the full **78.05 GB (`5,075` files)** completes in **~11 seconds**.
- **Post-Restore Summary Drawer, <1ms Background Purge & Self-Healing Chains:** Restoring any World State or Engine Core snapshot now automatically opens the **Time Capsule Snapshot Dossier** so you can inspect every restored file. Deleting or rotating old snapshots renames them in **under 1 millisecond** for background cleanup and automatically recalculates delta sizes across the remaining chain so no card ever shows `0.0 MB`.
- **1-Click Windows Cross-Drive Symlink Unlock & Safe Shared-Folder Deployment:** Want your Vault on an external SSD or secondary drive? Clicking the **Permissions Restricted** tile on Windows now triggers a one-time, 1-click UAC elevation that permanently enables unprivileged cross-drive symlinks. Plus, Sanctuary automatically detects shared game root folders and rescues any untracked mod files dropped directly into your `Mods/` folder into `Vault/Mods` before deploying.
- **Zero-Byte Mod Version History & Whole-Family Suite Rollbacks:** Every mod and script binary configured in your game's schema is archived into `<Vault>/Backups/Mods/` from Version #1 using zero-extra-byte hardlinks. Open **Version History** in **Your Vault** (or click **Versions** inside any **Mod Dossier**) to roll back an entire multi-file mod suite together via the **Suite Revision Matrix**, toggle **Keep Multiple** vs. **Keep One**, or enable **Auto-Swap Versions**.
- **Instant 279-Mod Collection Equip/Unequip & Dossier Safety Alerts:** Added a prominent **Equip / Unequip (`Add All` / `Remove All`)** button right in the **Mod Dossier** hero card and a sleek glass pill on the **Collection Contents** header. Under the hood, batch `O(1)` lookup indexing makes equipping or unequipping a **279-mod Collection** instantaneous while running full **Pre-Equip & Yeet Cascade** checks (Game Version mismatches, Missing DLC, Missing Dependencies, Flavor Swaps, Collisions, and Dependent mods).
