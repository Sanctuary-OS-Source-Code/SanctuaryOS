### Architecture & Performance Fixes
- **Homestead Lab Ecosystem Forge (`BUILDER`) $O(N^2) \rightarrow O(1)$ UI Freeze Resolution (`sanctuary-os/src/Lab.tsx`, `sanctuary-os/src/utils/logScrubber.ts`, `sanctuary-os/src/shared-components/dropdowns.tsx`):**
  - **Indexed `WeakMap` Mod Lookup Cache (`sanctuary-os/src/utils/logScrubber.ts`):** Eliminated the multi-second main-thread CPU lockup triggered when switching to the Ecosystem Forge (`BUILDER`) tab in Homestead Lab. Previously, rendering the Adversarial `<ModSearchDropdown>` mapped `getModName(m)` across every physical mod in `modList`, invoking `resolveRealReportMod` and `getModVersionLabel` multiple times per mod and executing $O(N^2)$ full-array scans and `normalizeBaseKey` regular expression passes across the entire mod library. Introduced `getModListIndex` (`WeakMap<any[], ModListIndex>`) and `getModBaseKey` (`WeakMap<object, string>`) to index hashes, IDs, normalized base keys, and family IDs in a single $O(N)$ pass with $O(1)$ per-mod memoization.
  - **Memoized Ecosystem Forge Dropdown Lists & Session Poll Guards (`sanctuary-os/src/Lab.tsx`):** Replaced inline per-render `.filter()` and `.map()` array allocations on `modList` inside `BUILDER` with memoized `builderBaseMods`, `builderSymbioticMods`, `builderPrimaryAdversaryMods`, and `builderExtraAdversaryMods` hooks, removed redundant `resolveRealReportMod` double-calls inside `getModName`, precompiled extension regexes outside dependency search loops, and added `handledTerminalKeyRef` + shallow state equality guards to the 1-second `poll_lab_verification_session` loop.
  - **Short-Circuiting `<ModSearchDropdown>` Filtering (`sanctuary-os/src/shared-components/dropdowns.tsx`):** Memoized `ModSearchDropdown` candidate filtering so closed dropdowns perform zero array iterations and open dropdowns short-circuit as soon as 10 unique matches are collected instead of constructing a full-library `Map` on every render.
