- **Zero-Permissive RLS, Spoke `anon` Helper Grants, Staging Payload Compression & FK Index Overhaul (`schema_os.sql`, `schema.sql`, `stagingCrypto.ts`)**: Replaced every permissive `USING (true)` operational write policy across all Hub and Spoke tables with consolidated single-policy-per-command ownership and staff clearance policies using `(select auth.uid())` initplan subqueries and non-recursive `SECURITY DEFINER` helpers (`SET search_path = public, pg_temp`), granted `USAGE` on `sanctuary_internal` and `EXECUTE` on read-only RLS role helpers (`spoke_user_role`, `is_spoke_staff`, `is_spoke_creator_or_staff`, `owns_mason`) to `anon` while keeping `runtime_secrets` locked down, raised `citizen_staging_queue` encrypted payload limits to `10 MB` alongside transparent GZIP pre-encryption compression (`CompressionStream("gzip")`), chunked base64 encoding, and single-flight deduplication in `stagingCrypto.ts` so multi-thousand-artifact Vault Companion snapshots sync cleanly without RLS `42501` rejections or redundant timers, indexed every foreign key, and verified zero Supabase security lints across both live projects.
