## Liquid Glass Surface Unification & Typography Polish

### Visual Overhauls
- **Workbench Category Pills & Numeric Steppers (`WorkbenchSidePanel.tsx`, `WorkbenchVisualEditor.tsx`, `WorkbenchTemplateBuilder.tsx`, `WorkbenchTemplateTools.tsx`):** Toned back the stacked specular sheen on the 18 category chips in `WorkbenchCategoryBar` by replacing heavy nested `.glass-surface` / `.glass-panel` highlights with a restrained low-glow glass border (`border-[color-mix(in_srgb,var(--text)_10%,transparent)]` inactive, `border-[color-mix(in_srgb,var(--accent)_40%,rgba(255,255,255,0.20))]` active), removed the cramped `rounded-full` `font-mono` count bubbles (which rendered slashed zeros) in favor of clean inline `tabular-nums`, and converted numeric stepper inputs (`Adult`, `Child`, `Elder`) from `font-mono` to `tabular-nums`.
- **Recessed Glass Text Inputs & Left-Edge Clipping Fix (`App.css`, `LexiconVisualStudio.tsx`, `SchemaVisualStudio.tsx`, `ThemeCard.tsx`):** Fixed left-edge text crowding and curved bevel glare on table-grid inputs by adding global `input.glass-panel` / `input.glass-surface` / `textarea.glass-panel` / `textarea.glass-surface` rules in `App.css` with a recessed inner shadow (`background-image: none !important`, `padding-left: 0.875rem !important`) and widened horizontal padding inside `LexiconVisualStudio` field cards and `DynamicLexiconPreviewBanner` 2x2 terminology cells.
- **OS-Wide Neutral Card Badge & Action Chip Conversion (`LexiconVisualStudio.tsx`, `WorkbenchVisualEditor.tsx`, `WorkbenchSidePanel.tsx`, `ChameleonControlDashboard.tsx`, `KeepersTitleAreaStudio.tsx`, `MasonProfileArtifacts.tsx`, `MasonPostViewer.tsx`, `TimeCapsuleSidePanels.tsx`):** Converted remaining colored-border/colored-text badges and mini action pills (`Sanctuary`, `Reset`, `Unsaved`, `Modified`, `Advanced`, `Auto-Tune Glass`, `Auto-Harmonize`, `Reply`, `Flag`, `Extract Selected`) to the neutral `.glass-surface` card badge style (`border-[color-mix(in_srgb,var(--text)_12%,transparent)] text-[var(--text)]`) with semantic color applied exclusively to the leading Material Symbol icon.
- **Visual Comm-Link / Release Notes / Directives Inline Code & Bold (`**`) Rendering (`ReleaseNotesShowcase.tsx`, `UnifiedPostEditor.tsx`, `MasonPostViewer.tsx`, `KeepersWebDirectivesEditor.tsx`, `WebDocumentFeed.tsx`, `MarkdownRenderer.tsx`):** Replaced old-style cyan-tinted `.glass-surface` inline code pills (`SidePanel`, `webauthn.dll`) and colored-border builder pills (`New Section`, `Add Card`, `Icon`, `Delete`, `Done`, `In Progress`, `Completed`) with neutral card-style chips (`bg-[color-mix(in_srgb,var(--text)_6%,transparent)] border-[color-mix(in_srgb,var(--text)_12%,transparent)] text-[var(--text)]`). Upgraded `renderCleanInlineText` so `**...**` (and `__...__`) is parsed before backticks, unescapes TipTap markdown sequences, renders `<strong className="font-black text-[var(--text)]">` at full 100% brightness (replacing parent `opacity-80` stacking with `color-mix` text dimming), and wired `renderCleanInlineText` into the Visual Editor (`UnifiedPostBodyEditor`), Directives Builder (`KeepersWebDirectivesEditor`), and Directives Feed (`WebDocumentFeed`).
- **Floating Rounded Mobile Bottom Dock, Fullscreen PWA Bar Fix & Unified Mobile `StatTileCarousel` Tabs (`MobileBottomNav.tsx`, `StagingPayloadEditor.tsx`, `Blueprints.tsx`, `VaultTabs.tsx`, `DbpfScout.tsx`, `TimeCapsule.tsx`, `CitizensWorkbench.tsx`, `SharedCommandScreenLayout.tsx`, `tabs.tsx`, `App.css`, `index.html`, `ThemeContext.tsx`, `pwaManager.tsx`, `sw.js`):** Replaced the flush edge-to-edge `!rounded-none` mobile bottom navigation bar in `MobileBottomNav.tsx` with a floating `rounded-3xl` 3D Liquid Glass dock (`bottom-[calc(0.625rem+env(safe-area-inset-bottom,0px))] left-3.5 right-3.5 h-[68px]`) featuring full 4-edge directional specular borders (`--glass-border-top/left/right/bottom`) matching the theme's corner radius and `Sidebar.tsx`. Replaced legacy mobile pill tabs (`HoverTabDrawer` / `MobileSegmentedControl`) in `StagingPayloadEditor.tsx` (Web Blueprints/Modpacks), `Blueprints.tsx`, `VaultTabs.tsx`, `DbpfScout.tsx`, `TimeCapsule.tsx`, and `CitizensWorkbench.tsx` with the shared `<StatTileCarousel>` and `<DashboardStatTile variant="tab">` formatted as a 2-up horizontal carousel (`sa-stat-tile-carousel`) on mobile viewports.
