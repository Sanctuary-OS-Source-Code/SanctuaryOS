- **Universal Drag & Drop Asset Installer (`json_bundle_intake.rs`, `jsonAssetDropInstaller.ts`, `useVaultIntake.ts`, `useWorkbenchFiles.ts`, `DropzoneSidePanel.tsx`):** Expanded Drag & Drop ingestion beyond mods/archives to auto-detect, inspect, and install Blueprints, `{Lexicons}`, `{Chameleons}` (including `.zip` bundles and co-dropped wallpaper/preview images), Workbench templates, and Master Schemas from any workspace view.

- **macOS Universal Darwin Build Stability (`vite.config.ts`, `monacoTheme.ts`, `ReleaseNotesEditorDrawer.tsx`, `package.json`, `.github/workflows/*.yml`):** Eliminated `vite build` `Abort trap: 6` (Exit Code 134) crashes on `universal-apple-darwin` by pinning Node 22 LTS + `--max-old-space-size=4096` across local builds and CI workflows, breaking Rollup circular chunk dependencies, lazy-loading Monaco and Tiptap editors with `slimMonacoPlugin()` stripping unused `ts`/`css`/`html` workers down to `editor.worker` + `json.worker`, and filtering editor chunks out of entry `modulepreload` hints.
