## 1. PREAMBLE & RIGHT OF MODIFICATION
This Sanctuary Global Compliance Protocol ("Protocol") outlines the mandatory moderation, safety, and content sanitization standards required by all officially recognized Sanctuary Partners.

**Living Document Clause:** Sanctuary Foundry reserves the right to modify, expand, or redefine Compliance Tiers, automated enforcement mechanisms, and malware definitions at any time to respond to evolving security threats or legal regulations. Updates become effective upon publication or reasonable notice, except emergency malware/security updates, which may take effect immediately.

## 2. THE FLORIDA COMPLIANCE LAYER (CONTENT SCRUBBING)
Sanctuary OS operates on the philosophy of "User Sovereignty vs. Network Integrity." While users maintain total authority over their local Vaults, the Global Nexus and public-facing endpoints must remain legally compliant and safe.

Partners must ensure their internal moderation teams (Architects, Oversight and Wayfinders) actively categorize all database artifacts according to the current Compliance Tiers.

### Currently Defined Content Tiers:
*   **Compliance Tiers 1-4 (Explicit, Illicit, Extreme Violence, Taboo):**
    *   **Definition:** Any artifact, mod, or asset containing adult themes, illicit substances, extreme violence, or taboo/sensitive content.
    *   **Local Sovereignty:** Permitted for local use by Citizens within their isolated Vaults.
    *   **Partner Obligation (The Scrub):** The Partner agrees that their Spoke Database must properly flag these assets. The Core OS will automatically scrub flagged Tier 1-4 artifacts from all public Nexus Feeds, community Search Results, and Cloud Blueprints (SNCXXX codes). Partners may not attempt to bypass these UI/API filters. Severe artifacts (Tiers 3-5) additionally trigger a Vault Lock restricting support operations.

## 3. MALWARE ENFORCEMENT & ZERO-TOLERANCE (TIER 5)
Sanctuary OS employs automated Rust-based heuristic and DNA (SHA-256) signature scanning to protect the ecosystem.

### 3.1. Compliance Tier 5 (Malware):
*   **Definition:** Any artifact containing malicious code, unauthorized executable scripts (e.g., os.system injections), or destructive payloads.
*   **Zero-Choice Lockdown:** The Partner acknowledges that if the Core OS detects Tier 5 DNA originating from the Partner's database, the OS will automatically halt game execution, isolate the asset into the Quarantine Zone, and lock the user's interface.
*   **Secure Shred & Telemetry:** To remove the threat, the OS utilizes the "Secure Shred" protocol. The Partner agrees that upon execution of a Secure Shred, anonymous Quarantine Manifests (File Name, DNA Hash, and Threat Signature) will be automatically routed to the Core OS Oversight Command to track and neutralize global malware clusters.

### 3.2. Partner Obligation for Tier 5:
Partners must not host, endorse, or intentionally allow Tier 5 DNA to propagate within their Spoke Database.
If a Partner discovers malware within their community, their Oversight/Architect team must immediately flag it as Tier 5 to trigger network-wide neutralization.

## 4. THE NUCLEAR OVERRIDE (SEVERANCE)
The Partner acknowledges the existence of the "Nuclear Override" built into the Core OS System Preferences (allowing advanced users to bypass security lockouts for local testing).
*   **Global Blacklisting:** The Partner understands and agrees that Sanctuary may mark the user account and/or their hardware signature hash as ineligible for network services. Hardware bans are exclusively recorded in a decoupled ban ledger at the time of the infraction, meaning we do not persistently track standard users' hardware signatures prior to a ban.
*   **Partner Network Impact:** Partners cannot request exceptions for accounts or hardware signatures marked ineligible under Sanctuary’s security procedures.

## 5. ENFORCEMENT & INTERVENTION
*   **Proactive Moderation:** The Partner is responsible for staffing an adequate number of Architects to monitor the "Scout Queue" and properly classify incoming crowdsourced DNA hashes.
*   **Foundry Intervention:** If a Partner consistently fails to classify Tiers 1 through 5 artifacts, Sanctuary Foundry (Core Oversight) reserves the right to unilaterally update the artifact's tier on the global Identity Matrix level.
*   **Breach of Protocol:** Repeated or intentional failure to enforce this Protocol (e.g., intentionally un-flagging malware or surfacing NSFW content to public feeds) constitutes a material breach of the Sanctuary Partners Program Agreement and will result in immediate severance of the Partner's Edge Gateway connections, identity-linking services, or other routing mechanisms.
