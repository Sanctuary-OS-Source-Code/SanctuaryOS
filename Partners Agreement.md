## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Agreement outlines the relationship, legal obligations, and financial structure between Sanctuary Foundry and the Partner.

By entering this Agreement, the Partner is granted recognition as an official Sanctuary Partner and may connect its game-specific workspace to approved Sanctuary network services, avoiding the need to operate a separate fork for official shared-app participation.

No partnership, joint venture, employment, agency, franchise, fiduciary relationship, etc. exists.

This “Partners Agreement” does not legally create a partnership.

***

## 2. PARTNERSHIP RELATIONS & RESPONSIBILITIES

To maintain a unified platform ecosystem, responsibilities are strictly divided between the Core OS maintainers and the Community operators.

### 2.1. Sanctuary Foundry Responsibilities
The Sanctuary OS Team is responsible for the overarching health of the platform and commits to providing the Partner with:
* **Core Maintenance & Support:** Ongoing maintenance, feature expansion, and priority support for the Core Application.
* **Infrastructure Support:** Master Schema support, Symlink setup, and Dynamic DB-Driven Expansion Schema maintenance/integration support as needed.
* **Community Web Hosting:** Hosting and maintaining the overarching Community Website.
* **Dev Build Access:** Providing the Partner's development team direct access to Daily Dev Builds to ensure compatibility prior to public patch pushes.
* **Direct Input:** Giving the Partner direct communicative input on the future roadmap of Sanctuary OS and priority access to the core Keepers (developers) for urgent issues.

### 2.2. Partner Responsibilities
The Partner assumes full operational control of their specific Game Workspace and commits to:
* **Asset Creation:** Creating and maintaining their own Dynamic DB-Driven Expansion Schemas, Master Schema, Lexicons, and Chameleons.
* **Branding & UI:** Retaining the core Sanctuary Logo, Title, and Subtitles within the OS. The Partner's Community Name will be displayed in the Side Bar under the Sanctuary Title Area. The en-Sanctuary and en-Default Lexicons and default Sanctuary Chameleons must remain included in the core application.
* **Administration:** Staffing and managing their own internal teams (Architects, Oversight, and Wayfinders). Sanctuary Foundry explicitly waives any right to dictate how community teams are staffed or governed.
* **Hosting:** Hosting and maintaining their own specific Game Database (the "Spoke").

## 3. LEGAL OBLIGATIONS & COMPLIANCE

### 3.1. Content Moderation & Feed Scrubbing
To protect the integrity of the Sanctuary Network, the Partner strictly agrees to enforce the Sanctuary Compliance Protocol.
* “Sanctuary Compliance Protocol” means the then-current written compliance policy published or provided by Sanctuary Foundry.
* Updates become effective upon publication or reasonable notice, except emergency malware/security updates, which may take effect immediately.

### 3.2. Legal Liability
The Partner legally acknowledges that they maintain full ownership of their specific Game Database.

The Partner is solely legally responsible and liable for all content, metadata, user actions, and database management occurring within their hosted database.

Sanctuary Foundry claims no ownership or liability over the Partner's specific game ecosystem.

### 3.3. Platform Audits
Sanctuary Foundry reserves the right to conduct periodic audits of the Partner’s database feeds and moderation practices to ensure this Agreement (specifically Section 3.1) is being strictly followed.

## 4. DONATION STRUCTURE & FINANCIALS

Sanctuary OS believes in zero-friction community support. The donation structure is defined as follows:

### 4.1. Partner Donations & Payment Processing
Citizens (users) are provided the ability to donate directly to the Partner Community within the Sanctuary OS App.

The Partner is 100% responsible for providing and managing their own Payment Processor.
* **Zero Revenue Share:** Sanctuary OS will not take any cut or percentage of the Partner's donations.
* **Chargeback Liability:** Sanctuary OS will not cover, mediate, or be held liable for any payment chargebacks, refunds, or payment disputes occurring through the Partner's payment processor.

### 4.2. Dev Build Donation Perks
Sanctuary Foundry manages a global "Daily Dev Build" unlock system via the shared User Database.

A donation made to any Sanctuary Partner will automatically unlock Daily Dev Build access for that user globally for the month.

This perk is managed by Sanctuary Foundry as a platform-wide incentive, but Sanctuary Foundry will not limit OS features to incentivize extra donations.

**Donation Verification.** Partner donation events may be reported to Sanctuary via approved API integration. The only data transmitted to Sanctuary shall be the Citizen account identifier and donation timestamp/status needed to enable or revoke Dev Build access.

## 5. DATA OWNERSHIP & SEVERANCE

### 5.1. Shared Identity Matrix
User authentication is managed via a shared Sanctuary User Database to allow single-account access across multiple Partner databases via FDW/API connections, identity-linking services, or other routing mechanisms.

### 5.2. Data Retention
Upon severance, Sanctuary may disable FDW/API connections, identity-linking services, or other routing mechanisms.

Partner databases shall remain independently hosted and shall retain their local workspace records, including any locally stored Citizen references, role assignments, and activity records existing before severance.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

During the term of this Agreement, the Partner may have access to unreleased "Dev Builds," proprietary atomic logic roadmaps, backend FDW/API connections, identity-linking services, or other routing architectures, and confidential platform security vulnerabilities.

The Partner agrees to maintain strict confidentiality regarding all non-public software, FDW/API connections, identity-linking services, or other routing mechanisms, connection credentials, and unannounced features of Sanctuary OS, and will not disclose such information to any third party outside of their immediate Wayfinder/Oversight staff.

This does not apply to public GPL source code, public documentation, independently developed knowledge, or information already lawfully public.

Access to Daily Dev Builds does not grant the right to redistribute unreleased binaries, credentials, private roadmap details, or non-public security information.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 9.1. At-Will Termination
This Agreement is entered into at-will. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, with written notice to the other party.

### 9.2. Termination for Breach
Sanctuary Foundry reserves the right to immediately sever the FDW/API connections, identity-linking services, or other routing mechanisms and terminate this agreement without prior notice if the Partner fails to uphold the content scrubbing obligations outlined in Section 3.1, or violates any legal obligations outlined in Section 3.2.

Sanctuary may immediately sever access for malware, illegal content, active security threats, or willful violations.

For non-emergency compliance issues, Sanctuary may provide written notice and a reasonable cure period.

### 9.3. Post-Termination Rights (The Right to Fork)
Upon termination, the Partner retains its independently hosted database and local workspace records as described in Section 5.2.

The Partner is explicitly reminded that Sanctuary OS operates on an open-architecture philosophy, and the option for the Community to independently Fork the OS remains fully available to them post-termination.

The Sanctuary OS code license does not grant ownership of Sanctuary Foundry names, logos, marks, or official network branding except as expressly permitted for active Partners.

After termination, the community must stop presenting as an official Sanctuary Partner unless separately authorized.
